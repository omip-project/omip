# OMIP Brand Guide

## Official name
Open Mission Intelligence Platform

## Short name
OMIP

## Tagline
Mission Data Infrastructure for Heterogeneous Autonomous Vehicles

## Vision
Enable every autonomous vehicle to speak a common mission language.

## Principles
- Open
- Reproducible
- Extensible
- Vehicle Independent
- Research Friendly
- Community Driven

## Logo usage
- Keep sufficient clear space.
- Do not stretch or rotate the logo.
- Prefer SVG for documentation.
- Use dark logo on light backgrounds and light logo on dark backgrounds.
